# Story: Commercial Lines Carrier - Call Center Help Desk

## Role Objective
Support brokers, insured risk managers, and claimants with accurate status and rapid issue resolution.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Service teams struggle to locate latest endorsements, audits, and claim communications.
2. Escalations increase when ownership and next steps are unclear.
3. Multi-party communications create inconsistent messaging.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Consolidates broker/insured interaction timelines across email and attachments.
- Surfaces pending actions, missing documents, and SLA risk warnings.
- Generates role-specific summaries for service reps and account managers.
- Improves handoff quality to underwriting, claims, and audit teams.

## Day-in-the-Life
A broker calls about delayed endorsement and claim status. The help desk agent opens one timeline view with outstanding items and responsible teams, resolves the inquiry, and triggers targeted follow-ups without multiple transfers.

## Outcomes
- Faster broker service response
- Fewer avoidable escalations
- Clearer cross-team accountability
