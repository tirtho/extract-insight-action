# Story: Commercial Lines Carrier - Underwriter

## Role Objective
Evaluate brokered submissions efficiently and maintain disciplined risk selection across complex accounts.

## Daily Pain Points
1. ACORD packets, schedules, and loss runs arrive with inconsistent quality.
2. Exposure details and safety controls are spread across PDFs and email narratives.
3. Referral decisions are delayed by manual evidence gathering.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Extracts payroll, class code, fleet, location, and prior-loss features from submissions.
- Highlights missing controls, unresolved recommendations, and documentation gaps.
- Produces risk summaries and referral packets with source-linked evidence.
- Prioritizes queue by expected premium impact and risk complexity.

## Day-in-the-Life
A manufacturing account renewal arrives with dozens of documents. The underwriter receives a structured risk file, missing-item checklist, and trend markers before first review, enabling quicker and more consistent decisions.

## Outcomes
- Shorter submission-to-quote cycle
- Better referral quality
- Reduced underwriting rework
