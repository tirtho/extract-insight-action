# Story 4: Reinsurer Viewpoint

## Scenario
Global Re Partners supports primary insurers with treaty and facultative reinsurance across property, casualty, and specialty books. It depends on timely, reliable ceded-loss information from diverse cedants.

## Core Business Challenges
1. Cedant submissions vary in quality, structure, and timeliness.
2. Treaty bordereaux production is slowed by manual normalization and validation.
3. Cat event aggregation requires rapid cross-cedant document interpretation.
4. Auditability and traceability are critical for reserve confidence and dispute avoidance.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Is Used
### Ceded Data Normalization
- Ingests claim narratives, ceded schedules, and supporting documents from multiple cedants.
- Extracts treaty-relevant fields into a normalized structure.
- Flags missing or inconsistent ceded-loss attributes.

### Bordereaux Acceleration and Quality Controls
- Validates submissions against treaty rules and expected data patterns.
- Produces exception lists with source-linked evidence.
- Shortens monthly and quarterly reporting cycles.

### Cat Event Intelligence
- Clusters losses by event, region, and peril from unstructured incoming packets.
- Improves speed of preliminary event exposure estimates.
- Supports faster internal reserve and capital discussions.

### Audit and Dispute Readiness
- Maintains source-to-summary traceability.
- Generates evidence packs for treaty interpretation discussions and audits.
- Reduces friction in cedant-reinsurer reconciliation.

## Day-in-the-Life
After a multi-state severe weather week, cedant files arrive in mixed formats with uneven completeness. The platform normalizes submissions, flags treaty-rule exceptions, and produces event-level rollups with linked evidence.

Global Re moves from ad hoc spreadsheet reconciliation to governed, auditable intake and reporting.

## Why This Matters
For reinsurers, decision speed is only valuable when matched with traceability and confidence. The accelerator improves both by turning document-heavy ceded workflows into consistent, reviewable operational intelligence.
