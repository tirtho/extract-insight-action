# Story: Specialty/E&S Carrier - Actuary

## Role Objective
Track emerging risk and portfolio drift in volatile specialty segments.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Data sparsity and non-standard risks limit early trend visibility.
2. Portfolio concentration shifts are hard to quantify quickly.
3. Pricing action often lags emerging severity signals.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Extracts non-standard risk indicators from inspections and claim narratives.
- Monitors drift by broker, segment, geography, and peril.
- Surfaces concentration and severity warnings earlier.
- Provides evidence-backed analytics for pricing and appetite governance.

## Day-in-the-Life
Before monthly governance, the actuary sees concentration risk rising in a niche segment due to recurring control deficiencies extracted from submissions. Leadership tightens appetite and adjusts pricing ahead of renewal accumulation.

## Outcomes
- Earlier drift detection
- Better specialty pricing response
- Stronger portfolio governance
