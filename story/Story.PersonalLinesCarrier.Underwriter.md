# Story: Personal Lines Carrier - Underwriter

## Role Objective
Price and select personal auto/home risks quickly while maintaining loss-ratio discipline and customer competitiveness.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Submission and endorsement details arrive in inconsistent formats.
2. Prior loss information is split across email threads and attached reports.
3. Manual review slows quote decisions and increases referral backlog.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Extracts applicant, property, vehicle, and prior-loss fields from unstructured documents.
- Builds a submission completeness score and highlights missing underwriting evidence.
- Flags risk anomalies and routes high-risk files to referral queues with evidence attached.
- Creates a structured risk summary ready for policy administration entry.

## Day-in-the-Life
A burst of new auto submissions arrives after a regional marketing campaign. The underwriter opens a ranked queue where files are already normalized, missing proof items are flagged, and prior-loss trends are summarized. Decision time drops from extensive document review to focused risk judgment.

## Outcomes
- Faster quote turnaround
- Fewer incomplete binds
- More consistent risk selection decisions
