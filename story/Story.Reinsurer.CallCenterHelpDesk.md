# Story: Reinsurer - Cedant Service Desk

## Role Objective
Support cedants with timely status, clear requirements, and smooth cross-team handoffs.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Cedant inquiries span underwriting, claims, and treaty operations.
2. Response quality drops when communication history is fragmented.
3. Escalation cycles lengthen when missing data is discovered late.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Builds unified interaction timelines across cedant submissions and communications.
- Highlights missing bordereaux fields, pending actions, and ownership.
- Produces concise role-based summaries for service and operations teams.
- Improves handoffs to treaty underwriting and claims analysts.

## Day-in-the-Life
A cedant asks for status on multiple submissions and exceptions. The service desk uses one consolidated case view to respond clearly, request only missing essentials, and reduce unnecessary escalation loops.

## Outcomes
- Faster cedant response times
- Reduced avoidable escalations
- Better service consistency across teams
