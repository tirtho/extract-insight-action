# Story: Personal Lines Carrier - Call Center Help Desk

## Role Objective
Deliver fast, accurate first-contact resolution for policyholders while reducing repeat calls and escalations.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Agents spend time searching multiple systems and emails for claim/policy context.
2. Incomplete communication history causes inconsistent customer answers.
3. Catastrophe periods increase wait times and customer frustration.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Builds a unified interaction timeline from emails, documents, and claim updates.
- Surfaces unresolved commitments, pending documents, and next-best actions.
- Auto-prioritizes vulnerable or urgent cases for faster follow-up.
- Generates concise customer-safe summaries for each interaction.

## Day-in-the-Life
After a hail event, inbound calls surge. Help desk agents open one consolidated case view with latest status, required documents, and promised callback dates. First-contact resolution improves and repeat-call volume declines.

## Outcomes
- Higher first-contact resolution
- Lower average handle time
- Better customer satisfaction during surge events
