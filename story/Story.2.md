# Story 2: Commercial Lines Carrier

## Scenario
Atlas Commercial Insurance serves middle-market and large accounts across workers compensation, commercial auto, general liability, and property. Most submissions and claim evidence arrive as unstructured documents from brokers, TPAs, legal partners, and insured risk managers.

## Core Business Challenges
1. Submission quality varies, causing underwriting delays and rework.
2. Premium audit leakage occurs when exposure documentation is incomplete or late.
3. Subrogation opportunities are missed due to fragmented file evidence.
4. Reinsurance and portfolio reporting require labor-intensive data reconciliation.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Is Used
### Submission Intelligence for Brokered Business
- Extracts payroll, fleet, class codes, loss history, and safety controls from ACORD packages.
- Flags missing items and policy-critical gaps before underwriter review.
- Ranks opportunities by expected premium impact and risk fit.

### Premium Audit and Leakage Control
- Ingests payroll and exposure evidence from insureds and auditors.
- Detects mismatches between declared and observed exposures.
- Routes high-confidence leakage exceptions to audit specialists.

### Subrogation and Recovery Optimization
- Scans police reports, contracts, and adjuster notes for third-party liability clues.
- Scores recovery potential and generates prioritized recovery queues.
- Assembles legal-ready evidence packets to shorten pursuit cycles.

### Reinsurance Reporting Support
- Extracts treaty-relevant loss fields and event tags from claim documents.
- Validates bordereaux inputs and highlights out-of-tolerance records.
- Provides traceability from summary output back to source files.

## Day-in-the-Life
A large fleet account renewal arrives with high document complexity. The platform pre-extracts exposure variables, identifies missing safety records, links prior claims to trend indicators, and surfaces potential premium leakage from prior audit variance.

The underwriter reaches decision-ready status faster, while audit and recovery teams receive actionable queues from the same document stream.

## Why This Matters
Commercial lines profitability depends on disciplined intake, accurate exposure capture, and fast, evidence-based operations. The accelerator turns unstructured workflow into measurable underwriting and servicing control.
