# Story: Commercial Lines Carrier - Claims Adjuster

## Role Objective
Handle complex claims consistently, control leakage, and accelerate fair settlement decisions.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Claim files include extensive legal, medical, and operational documentation.
2. Adjusters spend substantial time reconstructing chronology.
3. Subrogation and recovery opportunities are often identified late.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Builds structured chronology from reports, invoices, statements, and correspondence.
- Extracts liability signals and third-party indicators for recovery routing.
- Highlights reserve-impacting evidence and complexity markers.
- Prepares litigation/SIU handoff bundles with source traceability.

## Day-in-the-Life
A serious commercial auto loss includes multiple parties and conflicting statements. The adjuster uses a prebuilt evidence timeline and liability signal summary to make faster routing decisions and launch early subrogation review.

## Outcomes
- Reduced claim cycle time
- Better leakage control
- Higher recovery capture potential
