# Story: Personal Lines Carrier - Actuary

## Role Objective
Monitor profitability and recommend pricing, reserve, and segmentation changes using timely, credible data.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Key claim and exposure signals are trapped in unstructured notes and attachments.
2. Trend analysis lags because curated data arrives late.
3. Segment-level deterioration is discovered too late for timely rate action.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Converts claim narratives and supporting documents into structured severity/frequency indicators.
- Produces near-real-time monitoring views by state, peril, vehicle type, and channel.
- Detects drift in loss drivers and surfaces early warnings for pricing action.
- Improves traceability from actuarial output to source evidence.

## Day-in-the-Life
During monthly performance review, the actuary sees a rising severity signal for specific vehicle cohorts linked to repair-cost patterns extracted from claim documents. The team prepares targeted rate and underwriting recommendations earlier in the cycle.

## Outcomes
- Earlier adverse trend detection
- Better pricing responsiveness
- Stronger actuarial governance and explainability
