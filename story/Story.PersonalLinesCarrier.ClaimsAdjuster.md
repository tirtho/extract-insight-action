# Story: Personal Lines Carrier - Claims Adjuster

## Role Objective
Settle valid claims quickly and consistently while controlling leakage and identifying suspicious activity.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Claim evidence is fragmented across photos, police reports, invoices, and notes.
2. Manual chronology building consumes adjuster time.
3. Potential fraud indicators are easy to miss in high-volume periods.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Assembles claim evidence packs and event timelines automatically.
- Extracts contradictions, estimate variance, and injury/liability markers.
- Assigns complexity and fraud-likelihood scores for routing decisions.
- Prepares escalation-ready packets for SIU or litigation handoff.

## Day-in-the-Life
The adjuster receives a complex multi-vehicle claim. The platform presents a prebuilt timeline, linked documents, and anomaly flags. The adjuster quickly separates straightforward settlement items from SIU-referral elements and shortens cycle time.

## Outcomes
- Faster claim adjudication
- More consistent settlement quality
- Improved fraud referral precision
