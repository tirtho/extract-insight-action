# Story 1: Personal Lines Carrier

## Scenario
Horizon Personal Insurance is a national personal-lines carrier focused on auto and home. It handles high policy volume, high claim frequency, and intense service expectations from digital-first customers.

## Core Business Challenges
1. Claim volume spikes from weather and severe storm events strain FNOL and triage teams.
2. Rising loss costs and inflation pressure margins and increase rate sensitivity.
3. Customer retention is at risk when claim communication is inconsistent or slow.
4. SIU teams are overloaded because suspicious signals are buried in adjuster notes, photos, and correspondence.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Is Used
### Catastrophe Intake and Smart Triage
- Ingests inbound emails, photos, contractor documents, and policy communications.
- Auto-groups records by catastrophe event, location, and claim family.
- Prioritizes vulnerable customers and severe-loss cases for fast response.

### Customer Communication Reliability
- Builds a timeline of every inbound and outbound communication.
- Flags missed callbacks, unresolved service promises, and aging touchpoints.
- Triggers operational follow-ups before NPS/complaint deterioration.

### Fraud and SIU Prioritization
- Extracts contradictions and anomaly patterns across statements, invoices, and repair estimates.
- Scores claims for SIU referral likelihood and evidence completeness.
- Packages supporting artifacts so SIU can investigate faster.

### Coverage and Documentation Quality
- Detects missing documents and unclear policy/endorsement references early.
- Produces evidence bundles for escalations and complaint responses.

## Day-in-the-Life
A hailstorm produces thousands of auto and property claims in 72 hours. The platform consolidates fragmented claim evidence, routes urgent households first, flags suspicious repair estimate patterns, and creates regulator-ready communication trails.

By end of week, Horizon reduces backlog growth, improves first-contact clarity, and protects customer experience despite surge volume.

## Why This Matters
For personal lines, speed and communication quality directly affect retention. The accelerator helps carriers process high volume with consistent evidence, better triage, and more reliable customer outcomes.
