# Story 3: Specialty and E&S Carrier

## Scenario
Summit Specialty is an excess and surplus (E&S) carrier writing complex, non-standard risks. It competes on speed, expertise, and disciplined risk selection under uncertainty.

## Core Business Challenges
1. Each submission is unique, with narrative-heavy and inconsistent documentation.
2. Referral and exception workflows are hard to standardize across specialized underwriters.
3. Litigation sensitivity is high, requiring defensible decision trails.
4. Portfolio drift can occur quickly if risk signals are not captured early.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Is Used
### Complex Submission Normalization
- Converts varied broker packages, inspections, and narrative loss histories into consistent structured risk views.
- Highlights non-standard hazards, exclusions, and control weaknesses.
- Supports faster referral decisions with transparent evidence.

### Referral Governance and Consistency
- Applies rule-driven extraction and insight scoring for appetite and authority checks.
- Produces standardized referral packets with rationale and supporting artifacts.
- Improves consistency across underwriting pods and regions.

### Litigation-Ready Claims and Coverage File Assembly
- Builds chronology packs from emails, endorsements, and adjuster/legal notes.
- Flags contradictions or missing documents before counsel handoff.
- Reduces avoidable delays in complex claim handling.

### Early Portfolio Drift Detection
- Aggregates document-derived signals at class, broker, and geography levels.
- Detects shifts in severity indicators and concentration risk early.
- Enables proactive appetite and pricing adjustments.

## Day-in-the-Life
A broker submits a high-hazard manufacturing risk with scattered reports and historical incidents. The platform extracts hazard controls, prior events, and unresolved recommendations from inspection notes, then generates a structured referral file with clear risk rationale.

The underwriter escalates quickly with stronger evidence, while portfolio management gets near-real-time visibility into emerging concentration risk.

## Why This Matters
Specialty and E&S carriers win when they can be both fast and precise. The accelerator preserves expert judgment while removing document friction and inconsistency.
