# Story: Reinsurer - Actuary (Pricing/Reserving)

## Role Objective
Assess portfolio risk, support treaty pricing, and maintain reserve confidence with auditable inputs.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Ceded-loss detail is delayed and inconsistent across cedants.
2. Event aggregation and development analysis require heavy manual cleanup.
3. Governance requires clear traceability from outputs back to source evidence.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Extracts and standardizes loss-development indicators from cedant documents.
- Supports event-level aggregation with consistent tagging and timelines.
- Flags outlier patterns and data quality exceptions early.
- Maintains source-to-model traceability for actuarial review.

## Day-in-the-Life
During quarterly close, the actuary accesses normalized ceded-loss inputs and event-cluster summaries, identifies outlier submissions rapidly, and finalizes recommendations with stronger confidence and auditability.

## Outcomes
- Shorter actuarial cycle time
- Improved data quality controls
- Better reserve and pricing transparency
