# Story: Reinsurer - Underwriter (Treaty/Fac)

## Role Objective
Evaluate cedant submissions and structure treaty/fac terms with confidence and speed.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Cedant data quality and structure vary widely.
2. Exposure and loss evidence arrives as mixed unstructured packages.
3. Capacity decisions require rapid, reliable interpretation of incomplete inputs.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Normalizes cedant submissions into structured treaty/fac risk views.
- Extracts event, peril, and historical-loss indicators from source documents.
- Flags missing data and uncertainty hot spots before quote decisions.
- Produces evidence-linked summaries for underwriting committee review.

## Day-in-the-Life
Before renewal negotiations, a treaty underwriter receives normalized cedant packs with extracted catastrophe and loss-development indicators, enabling faster and better-governed capacity decisions.

## Outcomes
- Faster treaty/fac assessments
- Better decision confidence
- Stronger underwriting governance
