# Story: Specialty/E&S Carrier - Claims Adjuster

## Role Objective
Resolve complex and often litigated claims with strong evidence and consistent decision quality.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Coverage interpretation depends on nuanced endorsements and exclusions.
2. Claims involve long document chains and multiple external parties.
3. Litigation preparation consumes adjuster capacity.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Builds claim chronologies from legal notes, correspondence, and reports.
- Maps evidence to coverage language and highlights missing artifacts.
- Flags contradiction and escalation triggers for legal/SIU routing.
- Produces litigation-ready case bundles with traceability.

## Day-in-the-Life
A complex liability claim enters dispute. The adjuster receives a chronology mapped to coverage language and evidentiary gaps, enabling faster legal coordination and a more defensible claim position.

## Outcomes
- Reduced litigation prep time
- Improved claim defensibility
- More consistent complex-claim handling
