# Story: Reinsurer - Claims Analyst/Adjuster

## Role Objective
Assess ceded claims efficiently, validate treaty application, and accelerate fair settlement decisions.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Ceded claim documentation is heterogeneous and often incomplete.
2. Treaty applicability and claim chronology take time to validate manually.
3. Dispute risk rises when evidentiary traceability is weak.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Extracts treaty-relevant claim facts from narratives, schedules, and attachments.
- Builds chronology and exception flags for faster treaty validation.
- Creates evidence bundles for reconciliation and dispute resolution.
- Supports consistent decisions across analysts and regions.

## Day-in-the-Life
A high-severity ceded claim is submitted with partial records. The analyst receives a structured chronology, highlighted treaty-rule checks, and linked evidence, reducing back-and-forth while improving settlement confidence.

## Outcomes
- Faster ceded-claim evaluation
- Better treaty-application consistency
- Lower reconciliation friction
