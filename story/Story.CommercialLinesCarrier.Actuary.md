# Story: Commercial Lines Carrier - Actuary

## Role Objective
Guide portfolio pricing and profitability with timely, segment-level insight across commercial lines.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Exposure and claim detail quality is uneven across broker channels.
2. Loss development and severity shifts are detected late.
3. Portfolio drift by industry/geography is hard to see early.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Converts unstructured claim and underwriting files into structured rating variables.
- Monitors frequency/severity movement by class code, segment, and geography.
- Flags segment drift and supports early action on pricing and appetite.
- Improves traceability for actuarial assumptions and governance.

## Day-in-the-Life
In quarterly reserve review, the actuary identifies worsening severity in a transport segment linked to extracted claim attributes and litigation markers. Pricing and underwriting guidance is updated before renewal concentration increases.

## Outcomes
- Earlier profitability interventions
- Better segment governance
- Stronger actuarial transparency
