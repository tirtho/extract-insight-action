# Story: Specialty/E&S Carrier - Call Center Help Desk

## Role Objective
Provide high-touch support to brokers and insureds in complex, exception-heavy workflows.

## Daily Pain Points
1. Service reps handle nuanced policy language and endorsement questions.
2. Context is split across emails, binders, and claim/legal notes.
3. Escalations rise when communication history is incomplete.

## How Extract-Insight-Action Helps
- Creates unified interaction and document timelines for each account.
- Highlights pending exceptions, missing endorsements, and SLA risks.
- Produces concise case summaries for service reps and account leads.
- Improves handoff quality to underwriting and claims.

## Day-in-the-Life
A broker requests urgent clarification on an exception endorsement during renewal negotiations. The service agent quickly retrieves the complete evidence trail and provides a precise response with next steps, avoiding multi-team delays.

## Outcomes
- Higher service precision
- Lower escalation rates
- Better broker confidence
